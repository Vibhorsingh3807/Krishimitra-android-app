# Five Priority Crops Coverage

**Scope:** only Rice/Paddy, Wheat, Tomato, Cotton, and Potato.

## Rice / Paddy

Downloaded PDFs include:

- `NRIIPM_Integrated_Pest_Management_in_Basmati_Rice.pdf`
- `NRIIPM_Web_Enabled_Weather_Based_Prediction_for_Insect_Pests_of_Rice.pdf`
- `ICAR_NRRI_Direct_Seeded_Rice_Research_Bulletin_50.pdf`
- `ICAR_IIRR_Vision_2050.pdf`
- `DAFW`/ICAR cross-source material is recorded in the manifest.

Coverage: direct seeding, climate resilience, crop establishment, seed treatment, transplanting, fertilizer timing, pest/disease symptoms, IPM, ETLs, and weather-linked pest research.

Remaining gaps: a current full rice package covering all establishment systems, broader non-Basmati variety/zone tables, irrigation scheduling, and detailed post-harvest/storage.

## Wheat

Downloaded PDFs include:

- `ICAR_IIWBR_Wheat_Cultivation_in_India_Pocket_Guide_EB52.pdf`
- `ICAR_IIWBR_Wheat_Package_of_Practices_for_Increasing_Production_1984.pdf`
- `ICAR_IIWBR_Wheat_Variety_Guide_RB36.pdf`
- `ICAR_Wheat_Production_Research_Article.pdf`
- `WHEAT_ICAR_RABI_ADVISORY_2021_22.pdf`

Coverage: production operations, seed treatment, seed rate, variety selection, rust resistance, seed replacement, and Rabi advisory context.

Caution: the 1984 package and 2014 pocket guide are historical and must not be used as current chemical or varietal policy without revalidation.

Remaining gaps: current dedicated wheat disease/IPM manual, current irrigation-stage tables, and current harvest/storage guidance.

## Tomato

Downloaded PDFs include:

- `NRIIPM_Integrated_Pest_Management_in_Tomato.pdf` (Hindi, February 2023)
- `TNAU_Tomato_Production_Varieties_and_Package_of_Practices_Lecture04.pdf`
- `TNAU_Organic_Package_of_Practices_Horticultural_Crops.pdf`

Coverage: tomato identity, production and varieties, nursery/cultivation topics, insect and disease names, causal organisms, visual symptoms, confusable diseases, IPM, biological control, traps, organic horticultural practices, and label-claim material reproduced in the IPM bulletin.

Remaining gaps: current zone-wise IIVR variety/seed tables, current nutrient/irrigation schedule, and dedicated post-harvest documentation.

## Current regulatory cross-check

The `16_CV_DISEASE_REFERENCE` folder contains current PPQS/CIBRC PDFs updated 31 March 2026:

- registered pesticide formulations;
- chemical fungicide major uses; and
- biofungicide major uses.

These are regulatory reference lists, not permission to generalize a product across crops. Crop, pest, formulation, dose, and label/PHI must match the current official claim.

## CV disease-reference status

Tomato and Basmati rice PDFs contain useful visual symptom and disease/pest descriptions. Cotton has Hindi IPM material with pest/disease content. Wheat and potato CV-specific disease comparison material remains incomplete. No random image dataset was downloaded.
