## Offline versus online

Suitable offline candidates are dated technical bulletins, static production and variety guides, disease-symptom references, and historical statistics—subject to provenance and date review. Current pesticide registrations/major uses, live KCC records, mandi prices, weather forecasts, scheme deadlines, subsidy/interest values, and other API-backed data must remain online or refreshable.

## Licensing and reuse

The KCC resource page identifies the Government Open Data License—India. ICAR bulletins and journal articles show institutional or journal copyright notices. TNAU source pages show a copyright notice. Government PDFs may also carry publication-specific restrictions. The pack records research copies and source URLs; redistribution or commercial reuse must not be assumed.

## RAG and structured-data suitability

Best current RAG candidates are the ICAR-NCIPM rice, tomato, and cotton bulletins; ICAR-NRRI and ICAR-IIWBR production/variety guides; ICAR-CPRI potato material; and current PPQS/CIBRC regulatory lists. Best structured-data candidates are clearly tabulated variety, seed-rate, crop-stage, pest-threshold, and regulatory-use records. Do not convert narrative or dated recommendations into application facts without human review and current validation.
