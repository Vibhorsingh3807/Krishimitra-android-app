Tomato Varieties — IIVR

Tomato Varieties Developed by IIVR

1. Kashi Tapas (Hybrid)

Notification year2024 (SVRC-UP)
Gazette NotificationS.O. 1362(E) dt. 12.03.2024
Salient featuresIndeterminate; fruits red & oblong; avg weight 40–45 g; harvesting up to 1st week of June
Yield370–375 q/ha (summer)
Recommended stateUttar Pradesh
Special attributesHigh temperature tolerant (≈38±2 °C)

2. Kashi Adbhut (Hybrid)

Notification year2024 (SVRC-UP)
Gazette NotificationS.O. 1362(E) dt. 12.03.2024
Salient featuresDeterminate plant growth; fruits red & oblong; avg fruit weight 50–60 g; 5–7 days storability at room temperature (May–June)
Yield400–445 q/ha (summer)
Recommended stateUttar Pradesh
Special attributesHigh temperature tolerant

3. Kashi Chayan

Notification year2021 (AICRP-VC)
Gazette NotificationS.O. 1480 (E) dt. 01.04.2021
Salient featuresSemi-determinate; fruits red, flat round; avg fruit weight 100–120 g
Yield500–600 q/ha
Recommended statesBihar, Jharkhand, Punjab, UP, Goa, MP, Maharashtra
Special attributesToLCD resistant (Ty-3 gene); suitable for juice

4. Kashi Aman

Notification year2018 (AICRP-VC)
Gazette NotificationS.O. 261(E) dt. 16.01.2018
Salient featuresDeterminate; fruits round & firm; avg weight 80–110 g
Yield500–600 q/ha
Recommended statesPunjab, UP, Bihar, Jharkhand
Special attributesToLCD resistant (Ty-3)

5. Kashi Amul

Notification year2018 (AICRP-VC)
Gazette NotificationS.O. 261(E) dt. 16.01.2018
Salient featuresSemi-determinate; fruits round & firm; avg weight 90–115 g
Yield500–600 q/ha
Recommended statesKarnataka, Kerala, Puducherry, Tamil Nadu
Special attributesToLCD resistant

6. Kashi Adarsh

Notification year2016 (AICRP-VC)
Gazette NotificationS.O. 3666 (E) dt. 06.12.2016
Salient featuresSemi-determinate; fruits round & firm; avg weight 80–115 g
Yield550 q/ha
Recommended statesGoa, MP, Maharashtra
Special attributesToLCD resistant

7. Kashi Abhimaan (Hybrid)

Notification year2012 (AICRP-VC)
Gazette NotificationS.O. 2363 (E) dt. 04.10.2012
Salient featuresDeterminate; deep red & firm fruits; avg weight 90–100 g
Yield800–900 q/ha
Recommended statesJ&K, Himachal, Uttarakhand, Punjab, UP, Bihar, Jharkhand
Special attributesModerately resistant to ToLCD

8. Kashi Amrit

Notification year2006 (SVRC-UP)
Gazette NotificationS.O. 597(E) dt. 25.04.2006
Salient featuresDeterminate; fruits round & red; avg weight 80–90 g
Yield600–620 q/ha
Recommended stateUttar Pradesh

9. Kashi Anupam

Notification year2006 (AICRP-VC)
Gazette NotificationS.O. 597(E) dt. 25.04.2006
Salient featuresDeterminate; flatish round & red; avg weight 100–110 g
Yield500–600 q/ha

Yield400–500 q/ha

Cultivation Details

Comprehensive cultivation guide for tomato (Solanum lycopersicum):

  • Climatic requirements: Warm weather 20–30°C; frost-sensitive.
  • Soil type: Well-drained loam / sandy loamy soils; pH 6.5–7.5.
  • Sowing time: Rabi: Sept; Kharif: June–July; Summer: Jan.
  • Nursery & transplanting: Seedlings 25–30 days; spacing 60 × 45 cm.
Seed rate200–300 g/ha (OP); 100–200 g/ha (Hybrid)
Fertilizer150 kg N, 80 kg P₂O₅, 100 kg K₂O / ha; FYM 20–25 t/ha
IrrigationRegular; drip irrigation recommended
Weed controlManual 2–3 times; mulching
Harvesting90–120 days after transplanting
Yield40–50 tons/ha under good management
Post-harvestStorage 12–16°C; transport in ventilated crates
Major diseasesLate blight, Early blight, Bacterial wilt, Leaf curl virus
Major pestsFruit borer, Whitefly, Aphids, Thrips, Leaf miner
